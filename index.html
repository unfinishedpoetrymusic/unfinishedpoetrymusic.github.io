About

Unfinished Poetry makes alternative music rooted in rock, shaped by melancholy, and drawn to the tension between the familiar and what unsettles. Guitars and synthesizers keep their recognizable voices, then sometimes drift into hazier, less familiar forms.

Melancholy, anxiety, and panic run through Ragdoll, his newest album and most personal release yet. Building on the intimacy of 2023’s Goodbye for Now, it opens a more vulnerable view of years shaped by anxiety and panic disorder, and the uncertainty of whether those feelings will ever loosen their hold. In its more exposed moments, somber feelings open onto a fragile sense of peace.

Across Ragdoll, he searches for a fragile kind of beauty and the quiet relief of letting go.
